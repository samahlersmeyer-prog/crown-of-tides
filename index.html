    About

    An original fan-made adventure in the spirit of the classic Sierra quests of the early '90s. Five islands, 231 points, two endings. Point, click, and try everything. Dying is part of the fun, and you can always try again.

    With a mouse: move to the top of the screen for the icon bar (or pin it in the Control Panel), and right-click to cycle actions. Pick Help, then any icon, to learn what it does.

    Story, art, and code are original. Not affiliated with Sierra or any King's Quest rights holder.
